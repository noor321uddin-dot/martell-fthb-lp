Common questions

What does the Martell custom-home process look like from start to keys?

From land selection and design consultations through permitting, construction, and final walk-through, every Martell build follows a mapped-out sequence with a single point of contact for the buyer.

How long does a Martell home take to build?

Typical builds run 8–14 months from ground-breaking depending on the community, home size, and finishing package. The team gives every buyer a personalized timeline before signing.

Which of your communities is a good fit for someone building their first home?

Each community has entry-level lots and floorplans suited for first-time buyers. Get in touch and the team will match you to the community that fits your budget, commute, and family plans.

What is the GST rebate on new builds, and do Martell homes qualify?

The federal GST rebate for new builds can reduce tax paid on a qualifying new-construction home. Martell communities are built for eligibility; the team walks buyers through the details on request.
