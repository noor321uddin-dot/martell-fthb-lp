04
GST rebate

The GST rebate — asked and answered.

Buying new looks different from buying resale — and one of the biggest reasons is federal tax treatment on qualifying new construction.

  • The GST rebate can meaningfully reduce the tax paid on a qualifying new build.
  • Homes built in Martell Custom Homes' New Brunswick communities are eligible for consideration.
  • The Martell team walks first-time buyers through the eligibility process — no accounting degree required.
Ask us about your eligibility
Illustrative

How it fits your build

Federal and provincial rebate programs for new construction reduce the tax portion of a qualifying purchase. The size of the benefit depends on the home price, the buyer's situation, and current Canada Revenue Agency rules — which is exactly what your Martell advisor walks through with you before you commit.

This is educational only. Every buyer's eligibility is different, and figures change with policy updates.

Rebate rules and amounts are set by the Government of Canada and may change. Confirm your eligibility with your builder and accountant. See canada.ca.
