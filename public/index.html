08  —  The GST rebate

Ask about the GST rebate on new builds.

New-construction homes may qualify for a federal GST rebate — a meaningful consideration for first-time buyers weighing new against resale. The Martell team walks every first-time buyer through the eligibility process, so nothing about your first home comes as a surprise.

Ask about your eligibility

General information, not tax advice. Eligibility is confirmed with your accountant and your builder. Rebate rules are set by the Government of Canada and may change. See canada.ca.
